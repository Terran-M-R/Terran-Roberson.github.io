About Me

Hello! I am a rising sophomore after completing my first year at the UT San Antonio through the Coordinated Admissions Program (CAP). I'll be attending UT Austin in the fall where I aspire to be a professional in management information systems. I have a passion for using technology to solve business challenges and making work more efficent. Through my personal projects and leadership, I create solutions with measureable outcomes. In my first year of college, I learned invaluable skills and improved upon the ones I've developed over years such as resiliency and adaptability. After struggling in Calculus during high school, I'm proud to say that I have since earned an A and an A+ in Calculus I and II while at UT San Antonio. I've also managed to earn a spot on the UT San Antonio Honor Roll for achieveing a GPA greater than a 3.75. I am eager to learn and contribute to innovative, diverse teams through internships and campus opportunities while pursuing a career in MIS. Currently, I am a member of several technical field based organizations such as NSBE (National Society of Black Engineers), ACM (Association for Computing Machinery), and ASCM (Association for Supply Chain Management). I plan to stay active in the community as I prepare to transfer to UT Austin. Be on the lookout as I change the world!
